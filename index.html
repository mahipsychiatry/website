What I treat

Most of my patients are working on recovery from substance use, and most of them are also dealing with something else: depression, anxiety, trauma, a mood disorder. I treat co-occurring conditions together, not one diagnosis at a time.

Opioid use disorder

Buprenorphine (Suboxone) and naltrexone, started or continued by telehealth, with steady follow-up so refills never lapse.

Alcohol use

Medications that cut cravings and help prevent relapse, paired with a plan that fits your life and your recovery goals.

Co-occurring conditions

Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, PTSD and trauma, ADHD, OCD, schizophrenia and other psychotic disorders, and sleep problems, treated alongside substance use or on their own.

After rehab, PHP or IOP

Leaving a program is when people most often lose their prescriber. I pick up where your treatment team left off.

About me

Mahelet Yalew, DNP, PMHNP-BC

I'm a board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioner, and most people call me Mahi. Most of my career has been inside addiction treatment programs, at the partial hospitalization and intensive outpatient level, so I've seen how often people finish treatment and then go weeks without a prescriber. I started this practice to close that gap.

My visits are a conversation, not a checklist. Alongside medication I draw on motivational interviewing, CBT and ACT, and I'll be straight with you about what a medication can and can't do.

Common questions

Do I need to be in Virginia for visits?

Yes. You need to be physically in Virginia during each video visit. Anywhere in the state works.

Can you prescribe Suboxone by telehealth?

Yes, when it's the right fit. Federal rules allow buprenorphine for opioid use disorder to be prescribed by telehealth, and I can continue a prescription you started in treatment.

Do you provide therapy?

My focus is medication management. Many of my patients also see a therapist, and I'm glad to coordinate with yours.

Who do you see?

Adults 18 and older.
